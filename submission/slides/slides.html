The problem: two sensors, two scales

  • MODIS (1 km pixels) has the long history, since 2000, and is being retired.
  • VIIRS (375 m pixels) continues the record, since 2012.
  • Across the same area in 2012-2014, VIIRS counted about 3.8 times as many hotspots as MODIS. Splicing their raw counts would introduce an artificial break.
2 / 7Figure: method/figures/before_after.png (grey: raw counts; red: harmonized)

Our solution: one consistent series on the VIIRS scale

  • From 2012-02: observed VIIRS counts (Suomi NPP, then NOAA-20 from 2018-04). NOAA-21 is kept as a check.
  • 2000-11 to 2012-01: MODIS count × a ratio for that month of the year, learned in 2015-2025 when both sensors observed the same area.
  • Monthly counts across 376 active 0.25° cells, 2000-11 to 2026-10. Counts use detections inside the IBGE boundary of Rondônia.

How we know it works

We fitted the ratios on 2015-2025 and compared Terra and Aqua estimates with observed VIIRS across 34 statewide months in 2012-2014.

13.3%median absolute percentage error for monthly statewide totals
+0.6%bias in the combined statewide total
  • The protocol set before the test selected the seasonal ratio. The first regression had a +143% bias.
  • The ratio of NOAA-20 to Suomi NPP totals was 0.992.
  • The nominal 90% intervals covered 85% of monthly statewide totals in the test.

How NASA data makes it possible, and the limits

NASA FIRMS provides the hotspots

  • MODIS (Terra, Aqua) and VIIRS (Suomi NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) active-fire detections.
  • FIRMS country files (standard processing, 2000-2025) and the FIRMS area API (2026 and NOAA-21), clipped to Rondônia.
  • The IBGE boundary limits which detections count. OpenStreetMap provides the map background.

How to read the series

  • Available values from 2000-11 to 2012-01 are estimates. Terra-only estimates had higher errors.
  • Data end on 2026-10-31. The most recent months are provisional.
  • Grey marks missing data in the displayed series. Counts describe thermal detections, which can repeat for the same fire.
  • The ratios are fitted for Rondônia only.
