The problem: two sensors, two scales

  • MODIS (1 km pixels) has the long history, since 2000, and is being retired.
  • VIIRS (375 m pixels) continues the record, since 2012.
  • On the same area in 2012-2014, VIIRS counted about 3.8 times more hotspots than MODIS. A calendar built from raw numbers shows a false jump in 2012.
2 / 7Figure: method/figures/before_after.png (grey: raw counts; red: harmonized)

Our solution: one consistent series on the VIIRS scale

  • From 2012-02: observed VIIRS counts (Suomi NPP, then NOAA-20 from 2018-04). NOAA-21 is kept as a check.
  • 2000-11 to 2012-01: MODIS count × a ratio for that month of the year, learned in 2015-2025 when both sensors observed the same area.
  • Monthly counts on 376 grid cells of 0.25°, inside the official IBGE boundary of Rondônia, 2000-11 to 2026-10.
3 / 7Figure: method/figures/backcast.png (test years 2012-2014)

The user experience

  • Draw an area on the map or type its coordinates.
  • Read the year × month calendar: darker = more hotspots.
  • Grey = no data, never shown as zero fire.
  • Dashed line and "est.": estimated months. "prov.": recent near real-time months.
  • Compare a month with its historical median.
  • Download the monthly series, values per cell, the hotspot list (CSV) and the charts (PNG).

How we know it works

Test fixed before the results were seen: fit on 2015-2025, predict 2012-2014 from MODIS only, compare with what VIIRS observed.

0.6%difference in the total
0.998monthly correlation
13.3%median monthly error
  • A more complex model we proposed first failed the same test (+143%) and was not used.
  • Suomi NPP and NOAA-20 agree: ratio of totals 0.992.
  • The 90% interval covered 85% of the monthly totals in the test.

How NASA data makes it possible, and the limits

NASA FIRMS is the only data source

  • MODIS (Terra, Aqua) and VIIRS (Suomi NPP, NOAA-20, NOAA-21) active-fire detections.
  • FIRMS country files (standard processing, 2000-2025) and the FIRMS area API (2026 and NOAA-21), clipped to Rondônia.
  • The FIRMS missing-data list marks months with gaps.

Limits we show, not hide

  • 2000-11 to 2012-01 are estimates; Terra-only months are less accurate.
  • Data end on 2026-10-31; the most recent months are provisional.
  • Hotspots are not fires; no detections is not proof of no fire.
  • The ratios are fitted for Rondônia only.
