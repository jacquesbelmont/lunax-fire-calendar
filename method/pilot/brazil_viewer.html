Brazil pilot, 2024 (RA-11)

Local pilot, not the published app: one year (2024), observed VIIRS (NOAA-20 is the series, DE-11), FIRMS country files of Brazil and Bolivia kept inside the IBGE state polygons. It measures how a national version would load in your browser.

States are colored by their NOAA-20 detections of August-October 2024 (the core of the season). Click a state to load its 0.25° cells and municipalities; then load the detections of the chosen month as points.
