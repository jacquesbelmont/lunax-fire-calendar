LUNAX Fire Calendar

Monthly NASA FIRMS hotspot detections from harmonized MODIS and VIIRS records. Loading data…

1. Choose an area and a period

Choose a municipality, draw a rectangle on the map or enter its coordinates. Counts use detections inside the IBGE boundary of Rondônia.

Map

The map shows one month: the month of section 4, or the month you click in the calendar. Each grid cell is about 27 x 27 km (0.25°). Hotspots in the month colors the cells by their value (same values and colors as the calendar; the scale tops out at the 95th percentile of the cells). Compared with the usual colors each cell against the median of the same month in its earlier years, so it shows where the month was unusual. Grey cells have no data, which is not zero fire. MODIS and VIIRS detections are points at their real position; a number is a group of nearby detections of one sensor, so zoom in to separate them. Click the map before zooming with the mouse wheel. The layers button (top right) hides cells or points.

2. Monthly calendar

Each cell is one month. Darker colors mean more hotspots. Grey cells mark months with missing data in the displayed series. The dashed line separates estimated months (above/left) from observed ones. Years marked "prov." contain provisional months from FIRMS near real-time data. Hover for the month status. Click a month to show it on the map above.

3. Fire season

For each year, the bar runs from the month in which 10% of the year's hotspots had been detected (start) to the month in which 90% had been (end); the diamond is the month with the most hotspots (peak). Harmonized values, so years before and after 2012 are comparable. A year with missing months is drawn only if they cannot change the result. Whole months only: the data are monthly.

4. The latest month compared with its historical median

The data ends at its cut-off date, so the latest month shown here is not the current calendar month.

Light bars: estimated from MODIS. Dark bars: observed by VIIRS. Dashed line: median of the earlier years. Error bars: 90% interval. "prov.": provisional month (FIRMS near real-time data; the value can still change).

5. Downloads and briefing

CSV files cover the selected cells and period and include the NASA FIRMS acknowledgment and the data cut-off date.

The hotspot list holds the individual FIRMS detections in the selected cells and period. Count them first to see how many rows the file will have.
