Map

Choose what the map shows in the panel on the map (top right). The month views show the focus month (the latest month, or the month you click in the calendar or the comparison); their grid cells are about 27 x 27 km (0.25°), and grey cells have no data, which is not zero fire. MODIS and VIIRS detections of the month are points at their real position; a number is a group of nearby detections of one sensor, so zoom in to separate them. Click the map before zooming with the mouse wheel. The layers button (top right, above the panel) hides cells, points or municipal borders and changes the background: street map, NASA Blue Marble, or the NASA satellite image of one day in true color or in false color, where burn scars appear deep or bright red (NASA Worldview layer description). The image day is the day of the map month with the most detections; choose another day in the box on the map, or click a detection to see the image of its day. Satellite images stop at zoom level 9 (about 300 m per pixel); closer, they are only enlarged.

2. Calendar and fire season

Each cell is one month; click a month to focus the whole page on it (map, comparison, cards). Top bars: the median of each month over the period. Right bars: the total of each year with all 12 months of data. Red outlines: the fire season of each year, from the month in which 10% of the year's hotspots had been detected to the month of 90%, with a dot on the peak month. Grey: missing data, never zero. The dashed line separates estimated months (above/left) from observed ones; "prov." marks provisional months.

3. Same month across years

The focus month (outlined) against the same month in other years; click a bar to focus that year. Dashed line: median of the earlier years. Light bars: estimated from MODIS; dark bars: observed by VIIRS; error bars: 90% interval. The data ends at its cut-off date, so the latest month is not the current calendar month.

Season so far

Is this year running ahead of the usual? Blue: the running total of the focus year since the start of its tracking year (the month after the area's quietest month). Dashed: the median of the earlier years at the same month; grey band: the middle 80% of them.

4. Next season outlook (experimental)

How many hotspots to expect in August-October next year in the selected area, with the method that won a test fixed in advance on past seasons of the area's municipalities. Bars: what happened; diamonds: what the method would have forecast from earlier years only; the last diamond is the outlook, with the range in which 80% of this area's past seasons fell.

5. Downloads and briefing

CSV files cover the selected cells and period and include the NASA FIRMS acknowledgment and the data cut-off date.

The hotspot list holds the individual FIRMS detections in the selected cells and period. Count them first to see how many rows the file will have.
